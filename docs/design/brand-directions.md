# Brand directions (exploration, 10 Oct 2026)

**Outcome: C, Tide Clock, was chosen.** A and B and the earlier v1 screens
were deleted from the Paper file to keep it focused. The table below is kept
as a record. Current decisions are in `design-decisions.md`.

Feedback on the first direction: the colours and some of the layout felt
AI-generated and not like a brand of its own. Three alternatives were designed
(no longer in the Paper file).
Each has a brand sheet, plus Today (light and dark), Recording, Daily digest,
Search results and Review. None is final; the marks are placeholders.

What made v1 read as AI-generated: blurred pastel gradient blobs, frosted
cards stacked on frosted cards, a generic teal accent, and five similar boxes
above the timeline.

| | A — Field Recorder | B — Daybook | C — Tide Clock |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Idea | The app as a recording instrument | Each day as a written journal page | The day as a 24-hour dial |
| Colour | White, ink black, one safety orange | White, black, one cobalt blue | White, deep navy, buoy-yellow marker |
| Signature | 24-hour "tape" strip showing when audio exists | Masthead, digest as the lead paragraph, a pull-quote from the day | Radial day clock with recording arcs |
| Type use | Uppercase labels, huge date numeral | Large weekday headline, editorial body | Calm, rounded, numeric centre |
| Glass | Smoked-dark floating nav only | Frosted full-width bottom bar only | Clear-glass pill nav only |
| Reference apps (search these on Mobbin) | Teenage Engineering apps, Nothing Recorder, Just Press Record | Day One, Granola, The Browser Company's Arc Search | Apple Watch / Sleep apps, Oura, Structured |

The gradient blobs were removed in all three. Status still pairs words with a
shape (square LEDs, small caps, coloured bars).
